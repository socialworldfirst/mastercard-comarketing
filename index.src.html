Background

WorldFirst is producing a customer film about World Card. It brings together four World Card users from different markets, each describing how they use the card in their business.

Representatives from Mastercard and WorldFirst introduce and comment on the story. This makes the film a co-branded piece rather than a single-brand case study.

The purpose is to explain World Card through a trusted voice, and to build awareness for both brands.

Contributions

Roles and deliverables

What WorldFirst provides

  • Production and funding of the film.
  • Editing and versioning, including the short-form cuts.
  • Publication across WorldFirst social channels, the website and the newsletter.
  • A named project contact for the duration of the campaign.

What we ask of Mastercard

  • Agreement to appear as the named partner.
  • A contact through whom the Mastercard interviews can be arranged.
  • Brand assets, and approval of the final materials.
  • Publication on Mastercard channels. Optional, and the number is Mastercard’s decision.

What Mastercard receives

  • Named presence in the film and in the short-form cuts.
  • The master file and the short-form cuts, for use on Mastercard channels.
  • Distribution to the WorldFirst audience across social, website and newsletter.
