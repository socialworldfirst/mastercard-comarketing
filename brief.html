A World Card testimonial, with Mastercard

One film about World Card, told by the people who use it: four customers from four markets, each showing a different way the card works in their business.

Representatives from Mastercard and WorldFirst introduce and comment on the story, making it a co-branded piece.

WorldFirst produces and funds the film.

References

Mastercard: Accelerating growth for SMEs
WorldFirst: PRISM+ customer film

Contributions

WorldFirst provides

  • Production and funding of the film.
  • Editing and versioning, including the short-form cuts.
  • Publication across WorldFirst social channels, website and newsletter.
  • A named project contact throughout.

We ask of Mastercard

  • Agreement to appear as the named partner.
  • A contact through whom the Mastercard interviews can be arranged.
  • Brand assets, and approval of the final materials.
  • Publication on Mastercard channels, at Mastercard's discretion.

Mastercard receives

  • Named presence in the film and the short-form cuts.
  • The master file and cuts, for use on Mastercard channels.
  • Distribution to the WorldFirst audience.
